Amazon main image rules — FAQ

What background does Amazon require for the main image?

Pure white: RGB 255, 255, 255. Off-white, cream, shadows, or studio gradients can get the image rejected or the listing suppressed in search. MainImage Pass composites onto exact pure white and samples the edges to prove it.

How large must the image be?

Amazon requires at least 500px on the longest side, and 2000px or more enables the zoom feature buyers expect. We deliver every image at 2000px on the longest side (square 2000×2000 canvas).

Which file format should the main image be?

JPEG (.jpg) is Amazon's preferred format for main images. Every file we deliver is a JPEG, whatever you uploaded.

How much of the frame should the product fill?

Amazon's guideline is that the product should fill 85% or more of the frame. We scale and center your product to an 85% frame fill and report the measured value.

What else can get a main image rejected?

Text, logos, watermarks, props, lifestyle scenes, borders, and additional products in the main image are all against the rules. Our preflight covers the technical rules automatically; the report reminds you to confirm these content rules yourself.

My photo is small or blurry. Can you still fix it?

We will process it and upscale to 2000px, but the report flags sources under 500px as FAIL and under 2000px as WARN — upscaling cannot invent detail. For best results, re-shoot at 2000px or larger.
