MainImage Pass

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

MainImage Pass is a small online image processing tool run by an individual seller. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the tool handles and what happens to it.

What you send us

The only content you send us is the product photos you choose to upload (JPG or PNG files) when you use the tool. We do not ask you to create an account, and the tool does not ask for your name or address.

How your photos are handled

  • Your photos are processed on our server in memory only. Processing means removing the background, placing the product on a pure white background, resizing, and running compliance checks.
  • Your photos and the processed results are held only so you can download your ZIP file. They are not written to a permanent database and are not kept after the job ends. Because jobs live in server memory only, they also disappear whenever the server restarts.
  • We do not sell your photos or any other data to anyone. We do not use your photos to train models, and we do not share them with third parties except the payment providers described below, who never receive your photos.

Payments

If you buy a plan, payment is handled by our payment provider (Creem or Polar). Your card details go directly to the payment provider. We never see or store your full card number. The payment provider may share basic order information with us, such as your email address and whether a payment succeeded, so we can deliver the service and handle support.

Cookies and traffic measurement

This site takes part in the Ship or Die hackathon. Our pages include a traffic tracker provided by Ship or Die that measures visits to the site, such as page views and where visitors came from, so that traffic can be counted fairly for the competition. The tracker may set cookies or use similar technology for that measurement.

Where a consent choice is offered, we respect the choice you make. You can also block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not stop the image tool itself from working.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit) for security and reliability. We do not combine these logs with your photos.

Email

If you email us for support, we keep your message so we can reply and resolve your issue. We do not add you to a marketing list.

Your choices

Because uploaded photos are not stored after your job, there is normally nothing left for us to delete. If you have any question about your data, or want us to delete support emails you sent, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
