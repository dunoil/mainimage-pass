What the service does

You upload product photos. The tool removes the background, places the product on a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), resizes the image to 2000 pixels on the longest side, and checks the result against Amazon main image technical rules. You receive a ZIP file containing the processed JPEG images and a preflight report that marks each checked rule as PASS, WARN, or FAIL.

The report is an automated technical check. Amazon makes its own decisions about listings, and we cannot promise that Amazon will accept an image or restore a listing. Content rules that a computer cannot judge, such as props, text, or watermarks in a photo, remain your responsibility to check.
